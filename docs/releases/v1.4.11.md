## Changes
- **Bigger text:** the top bar's bank and patch names, and BROWSE's banks and patches, are larger and semibold.
- **Two cores:** Odin now plays on two of the MPC's cores, automatically: a second copy of the engine plays half the notes on another core, so heavy patches get about twice the voices before the voice budget steps in. Mono, legato and arpeggiator patches stay on one core. It adds 2.9 ms of latency, always the same, reported to MPC.
- **New ECO mode, Ultra:** runs Odin at half the sample rate (22.05 kHz) with a clean upsampler after it, for about 40 % less CPU than Lite on the heaviest patches. The trade-off is a darker top end (nothing above about 10 kHz). Choose it from ECO on the top bar.
- **Shorter names on screen:** the top bar and BROWSE leave out the "[author]" tag at the end of many factory patch names, so the names fit at the bigger size. MPC's PRESET menu and the Sounds browser still show the full name.

## Fixes
- **Silent patches in ECO Auto, Eco and Lite:** a patch using the Korg filter as filter 3 (EDM Synth Projektor, for one) could go silent from the first note, because the filter ran before its settings were worked out.
- **Faster when two Odins play:** Odin's random numbers (analog drift, noise) no longer go through a shared lock, which made two instances on different tracks slow each other down.
- **Bank and patch change together:** the top bar's bank arrows now load that bank's first patch, as on OB-Xd, instead of changing the bank name while the sound stayed. The bank name always shows the bank of the loaded patch, including after a pick from MPC's PRESET menu.
