## Changes
- **Bigger text:** the top bar's bank and patch names, and BROWSE's banks and patches, are larger and semibold.
- **Shorter names on screen:** the top bar and BROWSE leave out the "[author]" tag at the end of many factory patch names, so the names fit at the bigger size. MPC's PRESET menu and the Sounds browser still show the full name.

## Fixes
- **Bank and patch change together:** the top bar's bank arrows now load that bank's first patch, as on OB-Xd, instead of changing the bank name while the sound stayed. The bank name always shows the bank of the loaded patch, including after a pick from MPC's PRESET menu.
